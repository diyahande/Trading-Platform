# Selected Symbol Universe

The selected universe consists of three NSE-listed private sector banking stocks:

- AXISBANK.NS — Axis Bank Ltd.
- HDFCBANK.NS — HDFC Bank Ltd.
- ICICIBANK.NS — ICICI Bank Ltd.

These instruments were selected because they are highly traded Indian banking equities with reliable historical price data and are familiar companies whose price movements can be reasonably interpreted and validated.

## Symbol Selection

| Symbol | Company | Exchange | Reason for Inclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| AXISBANK.NS | Axis Bank Ltd. | NSE | Included as a major private sector bank representing the Indian banking sector. Its liquid trading activity makes it suitable for analysing price and volume behaviour. |
| HDFCBANK.NS | HDFC Bank Ltd. | NSE | Included as one of India's largest private sector banks. Its size, liquidity, and stable market presence make it a suitable benchmark within the banking sector. |
| ICICIBANK.NS | ICICI Bank Ltd. | NSE | Included as a leading private sector bank with different growth and market behaviour characteristics, enabling comparison with other banks in the same sector. |

## Universe Rationale

The universe was intentionally kept small to allow meaningful comparison and support defensible analytical conclusions.

The three selected companies:

- Belong to the same industry, allowing sector-level comparison.
- Are actively traded NSE-listed instruments, reducing the risk of incomplete or unreliable market data.
- Are well-known companies, making observed trends easier to validate against general market expectations.
